Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: August 1, 2026 · App package ID: com.cropai.tier · Developer: jjopensoftworks

Summary: CropAI is offline-first — everything you scan is stored on your device first. Creating an account (phone number + PIN) enables cloud backup and restore. We do not display ads, sell your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

1. Camera

CropAI requests camera access to show a live video preview on screen while you scan a field. The camera feed is never recorded, captured as an image, saved, or transmitted anywhere. It exists only as a live on-screen preview while the app is open.

2. Location (GPS)

CropAI requests your device's GPS location to query regional soil, weather, and solar data for your coordinates from third-party public data APIs, tag saved scans with coordinates, and center the offline field map.

3. Account, Phone Number & PIN

Creating an account enables cloud backup and restore across devices:

  • Your phone number (with country code) is stored to identify your account.
  • Your 4-digit PIN is never stored in plain text — only a one-way SHA-256 hash of it is stored.

4. Field Scan Data

Each scan you save (coordinates, soil profile, weather data, and crop recommendation) is stored locally on your device and backed up to our cloud database when logged in.

5. Device & Usage Information

CropAI records limited technical information — browser/device type, screen size, memory, language, and timezone — captured once per session or login to diagnose errors and monitor stability.

6. Data Sharing

CropAI sends coordinate data to public agricultural APIs (ISRIC SoilGrids, Open-Meteo, NASA POWER, OpenStreetMap) over HTTPS for field calculations. We do not sell data or share it with advertisers.

7. Your Choices & Account Deletion

  • Use CropAI offline/locally without creating an account.
  • Log out at any time from the account menu.
  • Clear local data via browser/device storage settings.
  • Request permanent account & data deletion: Submit a request on our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page or email jjopensoftworks@gmail.com.

8. Account & Data Deletion Requirement (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account Deletion policy, users can request full deletion of their account (phone number, PIN hash, saved scans, and telemetry logs) at any time without needing to log in to the app.
