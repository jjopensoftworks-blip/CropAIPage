📋 What's new

Changelog

A high-level look at what's changed in each release. For full build/release details, see the app's release notes.

1.1.3

Latest July 2026
  • Implemented mandatory registration and sign-in gate for accessing the scanner app.
  • Implemented automated 7-day session expiration requiring login validation.
  • Aligned TWA subfolder path structure to serve the app from /CropAIPage/app/.
  • Added a strict Content Security Policy (CSP) tag to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS).
  • Automated the deployment pipeline to copy obfuscated assets to public repositories.

1.1.0

July 2026
  • Added account login with a country selector, so your scans can be backed up and restored on a new device.
  • Added the ability to log out.
  • Fixed the camera/GPS permission screen sometimes getting stuck even after access was granted.
  • Fixed garbled text appearing in place of some icons and translated labels.
  • Added a clearer, more prominent disclaimer to exported PDF and CSV reports.
  • General reliability and data-accuracy improvements behind the scenes.

1.0.2

July 2026
  • Added offline map tile caching for the field map view.
  • Added a periodic background sync so saved scans stay backed up automatically.
  • Performance improvements to app startup and packaging.

1.0.1

July 2026
  • Added satellite-based vegetation health data to field scans.
  • Added certified-style PDF field inspection report export, with a verification hash.
  • Added CSV export of your full scan history.
  • Hardened account security: request signing, sign-in attempt limits, and rate limiting.

1.0.0

Initial release July 2026
  • Live camera field-scanning HUD with a floating soil/weather/crop data card.
  • GPS-based regional soil, 7-day weather, and solar data.
  • Algorithmic crop-suitability recommendations.
  • Offline-first local storage with background sync.
