Disclaimer

Last updated: July 28, 2026

CropAI is an automated estimation tool, not a certified agronomic service. Read this page before making any decision based on data shown in the app or in an exported report.

No certified inspection

All soil, weather, solar, and crop-suitability data shown in CropAI — including PDF and CSV exports — is generated automatically from regional satellite and open-data sources (ISRIC SoilGrids, Open-Meteo, NASA POWER) and general agronomic heuristics. It is not a certified soil test, and no agronomist or inspector has reviewed your specific field. Values may fall back to regional averages if live data was unavailable at scan time.

No responsibility or liability

No responsibility or liability is accepted for any loss, damage, crop failure, or financial decision — including loans, subsidies, or planting choices — resulting from use of CropAI or any report it generates. The app and its data are provided "as is," with no warranty of accuracy, and are used entirely at your own risk.

Verify before you act

Always verify CropAI's data with a qualified agronomist or your local agricultural extension office before making planting, financial, loan, or subsidy decisions. A cryptographic verification hash on exported reports confirms the document wasn't altered after it was generated — it is not a certification of the underlying data's accuracy.
