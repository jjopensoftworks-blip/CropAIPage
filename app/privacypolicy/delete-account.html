Account & Data Deletion Request

CropAI — Farm Field Scanner · App Package ID: com.cropai.tier · Developer: jjopensoftworks

Google Play Data Safety Notice: In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, CropAI provides all users with a web-accessible method to request deletion of their account and associated data without requiring app re-installation or in-app logins.

1. Scope of Data Deletion

Submitting an account deletion request will permanently delete all associated cloud data stored in our Supabase server database:

  • Account Details: Registered phone number and SHA-256 hashed PIN.
  • Saved Field Scans: Saved GPS coordinates, soil composition data (pH, clay, sand, organic carbon), 7-day weather forecasts, solar radiation values, and crop suitability recommendations.
  • Telemetry Logs: Diagnostic device profiles and activity log records linked to your account.

2. Data Retention Policy

Account deletion is permanent. All cloud records linked to your phone number will be purged from our servers within 14 business days. Offline local scan data on your physical device can be cleared at any time by selecting "Log out" in the app or clearing local browser/app storage.
