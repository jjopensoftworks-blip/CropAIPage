Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: August 1, 2026 | App Package ID: com.cropai.tier | Developer: jjopensoftworks

Summary: CropAI is offline-first — everything you scan is stored on your device first. Creating an account (phone number + PIN) is optional and enables cloud backup/restore across devices. We do not display ads or sell your data.

1. Camera

CropAI requests camera access to show a live video preview on screen while scanning a field. The feed is never recorded or transmitted anywhere.

2. Location (GPS)

GPS location is queried for regional soil, weather, and solar data from public agricultural APIs.

3. Account & Phone Number

Creating an account stores your phone number and a one-way SHA-256 hash of your 4-digit PIN for cloud backups.

4. Field Scan Data

Saved field scans are stored locally on your device and backed up to Supabase when logged in.

5. Account & Data Deletion Requirement

Google Play Account Deletion Notice: Users may request permanent deletion of their account (phone number, PIN hash, saved field scans, and telemetry logs) at any time.
